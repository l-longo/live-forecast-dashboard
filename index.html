Live Macro Forecasts — Can language models forecast the next official statistic?

Every morning a direct autoregression fixes a baseline for the next official release of eight US and euro-area indicators, and three language models say how far to move away from it. Each model answers three times: with no outside evidence; with a live social-media feed filtered by keyword; and with the same feed filtered instead by a language model that reads every thread and keeps what it judges informative. When the statistic is published, every forecast is scored against it. Everything is in real-time.

The next release

Pick an indicator. The chart shows its published history and, in the shaded zone, where each model thinks the next release will land.

The Fed's next move

The federal funds target range is set by a vote, not published by a statistical office: the forecast is a decision, in steps of 25 basis points. Each model answers twice — from the official series alone, and after reading the last seven days of US social-media conversation about rates, prices and the Fed.
